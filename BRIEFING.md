# Personal Website — Project Briefing

> Software engineer, writer, systems thinker.  
> Exploring intelligence, embodiment, healing, ecology, and technology.

---

## What This Is

A personal website built as a digital notebook — not a portfolio, not a blog, not a social feed. Something closer to a **cabinet of curiosities** or a **field journal**: a space that reflects the full range of thinking, making, and noticing.

The site should feel warm, textured, spacious, and intelligent. It should have humanity.

---

## Structure

### About

Not a standard bio. A personal, intellectual, interdisciplinary introduction.

Weaves together:
- software engineering
- AI-native engineering
- design background
- writing history
- notebooks
- psychology / neuroscience interests
- philosophy
- artistic practice
- healing / ecology interests

Should explain:
- why these domains connect
- what this website is
- what kind of exploration is happening here

Could include:
- timeline
- influences
- studies / certifications
- personal philosophy
- tools / workflows
- photos of notebooks / sketches
- a library section — books that have meant a lot

---

### CV

Professional identity, clear and navigable — without dominating the site.

Includes:
- experience
- skills
- AI-native engineering philosophy
- selected projects
- design work
- resume / CV download
- talks / articles (if relevant)
- tooling stack
- "how I work"
- **Engineering Philosophy** — one of the most distinctive aspects

Topics:
- AI-native workflows
- systems thinking
- cognition amplification
- creativity + engineering
- human-centered technology
- abstraction / orchestration

---

### Writing

Essay space. **Theme-first, not chronological.**

Themes:

**Intelligence & AI**
- cognition
- agents
- software engineering
- human vs machine thinking
- attention
- distributed cognition

**Mind & Body**
- nervous systems
- trauma
- embodiment
- meditation
- regulation

**Ecology & Care**
- gardening
- herbalism
- slowness
- rituals
- relationship with nature

**Meaning & Creation**
- philosophy
- creativity
- identity
- art

A single essay can appear in multiple themes.

---

### Feed

An evolving notebook / timeline stream. IG / Tumblr / Twitter / blog hybrid — **without being social media.**

Can contain:
- sketches
- poems
- thoughts
- photos
- notebook fragments
- diagrams
- links with commentary
- observations
- things being built
- quotes
- AI experiments
- interface ideas
- plant photos
- small essays
- snippets from journals
- reflections on engineering
- unfinished thoughts
- music / art references

Key principle: **low friction publishing.**
