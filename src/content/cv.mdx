<h2 id="philosophy">Engineering Philosophy</h2>

I believe software engineering is a cognitive discipline as much as a technical one. The best systems emerge from clear thinking, careful attention to interfaces, and a deep respect for the humans — and agents — that will use and maintain the work.

I am AI-native in the sense that I think about agentic workflows, LLM integration, and human-machine collaboration not as add-ons but as first-class concerns in system design.

## How I Work

I think in systems. I write to understand. I prototype early and refine iteratively. I value directness, intellectual honesty, and work that has a clear purpose.

I collaborate best with people who care about why as much as how.
