*"The limits of my language mean the limits of my world."*

Been sitting with this one for weeks. Not as a constraint but as an ==invitation==.

If language shapes what you can perceive and think, then **learning to say new things is a way of learning to see new things**. Every new word you acquire is a new slot in the perceptual lattice. Every concept you can name precisely is a phenomenon you can now notice when it occurs.

This is why I find writing clarifying in a way that thinking alone often isn't. Writing forces language into contact with the inchoate thing, and the friction produces precision — or reveals that the precision was never there to begin with.
