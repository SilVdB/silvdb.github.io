you weaponised hope
plucked it from the sky
and shoved it down my throat
right into my beating heart

it still flutters, sometimes
a tiny creature in a violent world
it looks at me
trembling, tired-eyed

I caress its tattered feathers
before I gently twist its neck
lay it to rest, finally
and let the grief wash over me
