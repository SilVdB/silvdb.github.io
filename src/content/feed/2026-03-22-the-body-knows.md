Your nervous system has opinions. Not metaphorically — *literally*. Before you've consciously processed a situation, something in you has already voted.

==The question is whether you're in the habit of reading the ballot.==

Most of the time I'm not. I process the situation, form a view, decide on a response — and only later notice there was a signal earlier that I ignored. A slight tightening somewhere. A reluctance I explained away. The body had data I hadn't gotten around to checking.

I don't think the body is always right. But it's **running on a different dataset** than the reasoning mind — one that includes a lot of accumulated history that doesn't compress well into thoughts. Worth consulting before you finalize the vote.
