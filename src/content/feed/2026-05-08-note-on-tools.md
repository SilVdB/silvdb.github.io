==Capability without legibility is just a more sophisticated kind of helplessness.==

I've been thinking about this with AI tools specifically. A model that produces good output but gives you no grip on *why* — no sense of what it's doing or how to steer it — doesn't make you more capable. It makes you dependent. The tool is driving; you're a passenger who occasionally approves the route.

The tools I keep coming back to are the ones that feel like **extensions of my own thinking**, not replacements for it. There's a difference between a tool that does something *for* you and one that does something *with* you. The second kind leaves you smarter at the end.
