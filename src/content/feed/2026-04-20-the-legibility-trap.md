description: "There's a trap in making things legible."
---

There's a trap in making things legible: ==the map starts displacing the territory==. You optimize for the metric, lose the thing the metric was pointing at.

It happens in organisations — you measure what's easy to count, and slowly *what gets counted* becomes what gets done. It happens in selves — the journal entry replaces the reflection it was supposed to capture. It happens in codebases — **the abstraction becomes the product**, and the original problem recedes.

The antidote isn't to stop making things legible. It's to remember that the map is always a lossy compression.
