---
type: poem
title: "Threshold"
description: |
  There is a moment before the thought
  arrives — a clearing
  where nothing is decided yet.

  I keep trying to live there.
  The mind keeps filling it in.

  But sometimes, in the gap
  between sleeping and waking,
  between question and answer,
  it opens again —

  and I remember
  this is what I am:
  the space that notices.
---
